Overview

RAKtherm is one of the PEX pipe ranges we stock at Buildora Materials, used on UAE projects for underfloor heating loops, radiant cooling/heating systems and general hot and cold water plumbing distribution. We supply RAKtherm PEX pipe in coil form from our Ajman base, with delivery available to Dubai, Sharjah, Abu Dhabi and sites across the wider UAE.

PEX's main advantage over rigid pipe on these runs is continuity: a single coil can run from manifold to outlet with no joints in the slab or screed, which matters directly for underfloor heating where an in-slab leak is expensive to trace and repair.
