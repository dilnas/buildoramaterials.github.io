Cable Ties & Pulling Springs

Nylon cable ties in all sizes; steel cable pulling springs

Description

Nylon cable ties in a full range of sizes for bundling and securing cables, plus steel cable pulling springs (fish tapes) for drawing cables through conduit runs. A Yuvix-supplied essential for any cable installation crew.

Available Brands

Yuvix

Available Sizes / Specifications

  • Cable ties: 100mm–600mm, natural and UV black
  • Tensile strength: 8kg–50kg
  • Pulling springs: 5m–30m coil lengths
  • Diameter: 3mm–4.5mm steel wire
